# Contributing

The Revenue Messaging Framework is open and improves with real-world use.

- **Found a sharper rule, a better scoring boundary, or a failure mode?** Open an issue or a PR
  against `methodology/`.
- **Ran it on a site and got a great (or a bad) result?** Issues with examples are welcome. They
  help calibrate the framework.
- **Keep the spirit:** specific over generic, proof over promises, buyer language over company
  language, constructive over dismissive.

This framework is maintained by [Colony Spark](https://colonyspark.com).
